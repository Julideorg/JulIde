# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want julIDE to be a project anyone can contribute to, regardless of experience
level, background, or identity. Everyone taking part is expected to help keep it that
way.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful. Disagree with ideas, not people.
- Assume good faith. Most misunderstandings are just that.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Remember that contributors are volunteers working in their own time.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or imagery.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions — and when
representing the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, contact the maintainer at the
address on the [@sinisterMage](https://github.com/sinisterMage) GitHub profile.

Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers will review and respond in a way they
judge appropriate to the circumstances, which may include a warning, temporary
restriction, or a permanent ban.
